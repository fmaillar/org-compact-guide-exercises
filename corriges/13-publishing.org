* Exemple minimal autonome
Le fragment suivant illustre la syntaxe utile, sans prétendre constituer
l’unique solution ni modifier automatiquement les ateliers.

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
(setq org-publish-project-alist
      '(("cours-html"
         :base-directory "/chemin/absolu/ateliers/site/"
         :base-extension "org"
         :publishing-directory "/chemin/absolu/exports/site/"
         :publishing-function org-html-publish-to-html)
        ("cours-assets"
         :base-directory "/chemin/absolu/assets/"
         :base-extension "css\|svg"
         :publishing-directory "/chemin/absolu/exports/site/"
         :publishing-function org-publish-attachment)
        ("cours" :components ("cours-html" "cours-assets"))))

# Le setup/init.el fournit une configuration équivalente et portable.
#+END_EXAMPLE
